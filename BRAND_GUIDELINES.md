# NIXON CHICKEN
**Brand Identity & Style Guide**

## 1. Brand Identity Overview
- **Name:** Nixon Chicken
- **Category:** Absurdist American satire magazine
- **Tagline:** Suits on the Ground
- **Brand Voice:** Authoritative, deadpan, institutional. The presentation is dead serious; the content is completely unhinged.
- **Concept:** A fictional political universe populated by characters obviously inspired by real public figures but never depicting them directly. Treated visually and tonally as a legacy print publication.

## 2. Color Palette
The brand colors evoke heritage editorial publications with a slightly ominous, institutional undertone.

- **Deep Red:** `#8B0000` (Used for the masthead, primary accents, alerts, and borders)
- **Cream / Off-White:** `#F6F4EB` (Used for background textures, newsprint feel—stark white should be avoided)
- **Ink Black:** `#1A1A1A` (Used for primary typography, editorial body copy, and deep shadows)
- **Slate Accent (Optional):** `#4A5568` (Used for metadata, datelines, and secondary captions)

## 3. Typography Recommendations
*Rule of Thumb: Serif throughout. The goal is to aggressively mimic a legacy editorial magazine (e.g., The Atlantic, Harper's, The New Yorker).*

- **Masthead & Primary Headings (Display):** 
  - **Playfair Display** (Elegant, high-contrast, classic)
  - **Cinzel** (Stately, Roman proportions, institutional)
- **Article Titles & Subheadings:** 
  - **Merriweather** (Sturdy, highly legible, print-like)
  - **Georgia** (A reliable web-safe fallback with excellent editorial feel)
- **Body Copy:** 
  - **Lora** (Contemporary but deeply rooted in traditional print)
  - **PT Serif** (Excellent readability for long-form satirical essays)
- **Captions, Eyebrows & Metadata:** 
  - **Source Serif Pro** (Use in all-caps, tracked out slightly for column headers and datelines)

## 4. Visual Style Guide: The "Forced Perspective" System
Nixon Chicken’s signature visual joke relies on a strict **forced perspective rule** applied to article header imagery.

- **The Rule:** The primary subject (the politician, pundit, or military figure) is rendered disproportionately huge and in sharp focus in the extreme foreground, set against a miniaturized, contextual background.
- **The Execution:** Think macro-photography or portrait photography placed in a dollhouse constraint. The background context completes the satirical punchline. 
- **Examples:**
  - A giant portrait of a sweating senator resting on a tiny fireplace mantle.
  - A massive four-star general towering over a tiny, 1/12th scale army cot.
  - A booming pundit’s face squeezed onto a tiny 1990s 12-inch CRT TV sitting in a swamp.

## 5. Prompt Framework for Forced Perspective Images
To generate consistent imagery matching the forced perspective rule, use the following prompt template structure and guidelines:

**Core Framework Rules:**
- **Explicit Camera Position:** The forced perspective mechanic must specify explicit camera position — the camera should be positioned inches from the foreground framing device at an extreme low or close angle, creating a natural optical scale differential rather than simulated blur.
- **Background Focus:** The background must be described as fully visible and in reasonable focus but genuinely small due to camera proximity, not depth of field.
- **Fictional Subjects:** Every prompt must include the instruction that all subjects are completely fictional and anonymous with no resemblance to any real living or historical public figure.

**[Photography Style & Fictional Disclaimer] + [Specific Camera Position & Framing Device] + [Foreground Subject Description & Scale Rule] + [Background Context in Focus but Tiny] + [Color & Lighting]**

### Standard Template:
> "A photorealistic, legacy magazine editorial photograph. All subjects are completely fictional and anonymous with no resemblance to any real living or historical public figure. The camera is positioned inches away from a [framing device] at an extreme [low/close] angle. Placed disproportionately large in the extreme foreground is [describe the subject: e.g., the head and shoulders of a sweating politician]. The subject is huge, creating a forced perspective and natural optical scale differential. The background is [describe the tiny background setting], which is fully visible and in reasonable focus but genuinely small due to the extreme camera proximity, rather than simulated blur or depth of field. [Lighting/Color: e.g., deep red and cream color grading, dramatic studio lighting, gritty 35mm film aesthetic]."

### Example Prompts:

**1. The Mantle Portrait (Framing Device):**
> "A surreal photorealistic legacy magazine editorial photograph. All subjects are completely fictional and anonymous with no resemblance to any real living or historical public figure. The camera is positioned inches from a heavy ornate picture frame resting on a wooden fireplace mantle, taken at an extreme low angle. Crammed inside the foreground frame, disproportionately large, is the sweating face of an older male politician in a dark suit. The background features the rest of a miniature, cluttered living room, which is fully visible and in reasonable focus but genuinely small due to the camera's extreme proximity to the mantle, creating a natural optical scale differential rather than simulated blur. Deep red, cream, and ink black color grading, harsh flash."

**2. The Vintage CRT TV (Framing Device):**
> "A surreal photorealistic legacy magazine editorial photograph. All subjects are completely fictional and anonymous with no resemblance to any real living or historical public figure. The camera is positioned inches from the curved glass screen of a vintage 1990s 12-inch CRT television sitting on the ground, taken at an extreme close angle. Squeezed onto the screen in the extreme foreground is the booming face of an angry pundit. The background is a dense, murky swamp environment that is fully visible and in reasonable focus, but genuinely small due to the camera's close proximity to the TV screen, creating a natural optical scale differential rather than simulated blur. 35mm film grain, muted greens, deep reds, and off-white colors."

## 6. Hero Animation Conceptual Storyboard
The hero animation establishes the absurdist tone exactly. It transitions from a hyper-specific domestic setting to an iconic seat of power.

**Frame 1:** A warm, slightly grimy **1970s American working-class kitchen.** Avocado green appliances, dark wood veneer cabinets, patterned linoleum flooring. On the Formica counter sits a steaming, generic mid-century casserole dish. The dish is completely unlabeled. The lighting is warm tungsten mixed with natural light. Soft Kodachrome film aesthetic.

**Frame 2 (Transition):** The camera pushes intimately in on the casserole dish. The warm, nostalgic kitchen lighting abruptly fades to a harsh, bureaucratic blue/white and deep red color palette.

**Frame 3 & 4 (Action):** The camera violently pulls back. The exact same casserole dish is now sitting on the seal of the Presidential carpet on the floor of the Oval Office. It is untouched and in perfectly sharp focus. In the background, visible but receding naturally into the background due to forced perspective distance, is absolute political chaos—figures in suits running frantically, papers flying through the air, overturned chairs. The tone is a deadpan political thriller.

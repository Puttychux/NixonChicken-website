Idea for Nixon Chicken promo animations

A chicken, wearing a vintage-style press hat is seen in a chair, from behind, writing at a desk in a dimly lit hotel room by a window. The camera swaps to his eyes and the sound of the writing is louder. Back and forth between his eyes and the page, both his eyes and the scribbling intensifying. He soon explodes in a poof, complete with a classic “egg song” sound. When the feathers fall, they spell “Nixon Chicken.” Then a gust of wind comes through the window and scatters some of them away, leaving “Est. 2026” in the remaining feathers.
Using a less expensive, but still visually worthwhile sketch-style approach, how much might this cost on average?
